Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Cancellation

PaperBanao Pro is a one-time 30-day purchase — it does not auto-renew, so there's nothing to cancel. Your Pro access simply ends after 30 days unless you choose to purchase another period.

Refunds

Because Pro access is unlocked immediately on payment, refund requests are handled as follows:

  • If you were charged due to a technical error (e.g., duplicate payment, payment deducted but Pro not activated), we'll refund the full amount within 5–7 business days of your request.
  • If you request a refund within 24 hours of purchase and have not generated any question papers using your Pro access, we'll issue a full refund.
  • Beyond 24 hours, or once Pro-tier papers have been generated, purchases are non-refundable, since the service has already been delivered.

How to Request a Refund

Email sk142464@gmail.com with your registered username/email and the payment ID shown after checkout. We'll respond within 24–48 hours.

Refund Method

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
