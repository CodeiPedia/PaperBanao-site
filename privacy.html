Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information PaperBanao collects and how it's used. We collect only what's needed to run the Service.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: username, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your plain-text password)
  • Usage data: number of papers generated and your subscription status
  • Content you provide: syllabus topics, uploaded PDFs, and question preferences used to generate papers
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Razorpay — we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate your account, generate question papers, process payments, send password-reset codes, and improve the Service.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google's Gemini AI to generate question paper content, Razorpay to process payments, and Supabase to store account data securely. Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing, never in plain text. Data is stored with Supabase using access controls limited to our application.

5. Your Rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account by emailing sk142464@gmail.com.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we'll remove your data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain payment records for legal/accounting purposes.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email sk142464@gmail.com.
