Frequently asked questions

What exams does PaperBanao support?

RRB (ALP, NTPC, Group D), SSC (CGL, CHSL), UPSC, Banking exams, and school-level papers (BSEB, CBSE, ICSE patterns). You control the subject and topics either way.

Do I need to know how to use AI tools?

No. You fill in a simple form (subject, class, topics, question counts) or upload a photo — PaperBanao handles the rest.

Can I edit the questions after they're generated?

Yes. Every question is editable by hand, and you can regenerate any single question you don't like without redoing the whole paper.

No. It's a one-time 30-day purchase. You choose if and when to buy another 30-day period.
